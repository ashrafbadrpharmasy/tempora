Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains how Tempora handles information when you use this website.

Files and browser-based tools

For tools that run in your browser, files you select are intended to be processed on your device and the resulting files are generated in your browser. Do not upload files containing confidential or sensitive information unless you have independently confirmed how the particular tool works. Some features may rely on third-party libraries or services; the relevant feature may not work if those services are unavailable.

Temporary email

The temporary email feature uses an external email service provider, currently MailboxTemp, to create inboxes and retrieve messages. Email addresses and messages handled by that service are subject to the provider's systems and policies. Do not use temporary inboxes for sensitive information, account recovery, financial accounts, or anything you need to keep.

Cookies and advertising

Tempora may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads, subject to your settings, applicable consent requirements, and Google's policies. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. You can learn how Google uses information from partner sites at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services, and manage ad personalization at Google Ads Settings.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider and third-party services may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, request logs, and security-related data as needed to deliver and protect the service. Tempora does not ask you to create an account to use the browser-based file tools.

Third-party services

Links and integrations to third-party services are governed by those providers' own privacy policies. Tempora does not control their independent data practices.

Children's privacy

Tempora is a general-purpose utility website and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or its features change. The latest version will be published on this page.
